PicklePro update (Nov 12): what changed and how to show it

It's live on picklepro.kirkorino.com and on the cleanversion branch (commits 15b0162, b9e701a, 28c9336). The database update (upgrade 17) has already been run.
To demo, sign in with an account that has at least one analyzed video. Use phone width for items 1–3 (on a computer: the browser's phone view).

1. Real names on the video
   Where: open a session with an analyzed video → the video.
   What: the overlay says the player's first name instead of "You", the partner's name, and "Opponent 1 / 2" instead of "Player 3". Names come from the session's players. Opponents get a real name only when there is one, because the video can't tell two opponents apart.
   Demo: play the video and point at the name tags and the shot labels ("Kirk: Drop?").

2. Lighter video overlay on phones
   Where: same video, on a phone.
   What: corner brackets instead of full boxes, small name tags, and the shot name in a bar under the video instead of on top of the court.
   Demo: play the video on a phone and show the "Latest shot" bar under it.

3. Court map on the Position tab (always shown now)
   Where: session page → report tabs → Position.
   What: a top-view heatmap of where the player moved (blue = passed through, red = stayed), with "Home base" on their busiest spot. Under it:
   - Kitchen / No-man's land / Baseline buttons: tap one (or tap the court) to highlight it and read a short tip with the player's own numbers.
   - A level from time at the kitchen (Baseliner, Climber, Net player, Kitchen boss) with progress to the next one.
   - Badges ("Line holder", "Net player", "Kitchen boss").
   - A "Next game" challenge card with "Add court positioning to my goals".
   - The other players' zone bars, folded under "Where the other players stood".
   Demo: open Position, tap No-man's land, read the tip, scroll to the challenge and tap the goal button.
   Note: the level cut-offs (45%, 60%) and the 35% challenge are starting values for a coach to tune.

4. Every rating is now out of 5
   Where: sign-up skill ratings, the after-game rating (Rate & plan) and the Journal's post-game step.
   What: shots (serve, return, drop, dinking, volleys) used to ask "Out of 10, how many went in?". They're now rated 1–5 like every other skill, and each number shows a description ("4/5 · Deep and steady"). Effort ("How hard was it?") went from 1–10 to 1–5: Easy, Light, Moderate, Hard, All out. Old effort scores were converted. You still rate by pressing and holding the pickleball.
   Demo: log a game, rate Serve by holding the pickleball, then show Effort in the Journal.

5. Recovery mode switch (was the injury checkbox)
   Where: Home (bottom), Settings, a session's Journal → Post-game, and the recovery card when it's on.
   What: one "Recovery mode" on/off switch everywhere. When it's on, goals pause, the player is hidden from Buddy, and their plan becomes a recovery plan. Turning the switch off ends it (the old "I'm back" button is gone). The app no longer says "injured" anywhere players see.
   Demo: on Home, scroll down, turn Recovery mode on, and show the card at the top ("Goals paused", "Hidden from Buddy") and Progress ("paused for recovery mode"). Turn it off again.
   Note: don't leave it on for a real account; it hides that player from Buddy.

Designed but not built yet (mockups only)
- New session flow: pick "Rate my own game" or "Analyze a video", then the session is named and timed automatically.
- Sessions list grouped by week, with start times.
- Skill scores backed by evidence (video, court tests, partner) instead of self-rating alone.
- Progress page: staying as it is for now.
