1 · Name and story

Every session sharpens your game

Hasa is Tagalog for sharpening or training; hasâ means honed, highly skilled. The app's job is to take a player's last session and tell them what to sharpen next: focus areas, drills and a practice plan, with progress tracked over time and optional video court-position analysis.

The brand is a good club coach: credible to adult players, encouraging without being cute. The pickle coach brings the warmth; the name, type and layout carry the credibility.

Writing the name

  • hasa in lowercase in the logo only.
  • Hasa in sentences: "Log your session in Hasa."
  • Descriptor when needed: "Hasa, pickleball coaching".
  • Never "HASA", "Hasa App" or "Hasa Pickleball".
  • Tagline, used as written: Know what to practice next.
2 · Voice

4 · The coach

A pickle that plays

One character geometry is shared by the mascot, the app icon and every lockup. Flat fills only: no outlines, gradients, shading or ground shadow.

Master pose: ready stance

Anatomy

  • Straight cylindrical body; stem nub leaning slightly right.
  • Bumps on the silhouette edge only, never dark spots inside the body.
  • Paper-coloured sweatband; oval eyes with highlights, short brows, closed smile.
  • Limbs in ink: arms 13, legs 15 at master size. Knees bent, feet level, paddle up and forward.

Where it appears

  • Yes: onboarding, empty states, milestones, the drill of the week.
  • No: inside data views, next to errors, in the header, or on every screen. One coach per screen at most.
  • On dark backgrounds, set the full coach on a paper disc (#F1ECDC) so the ink limbs and paddle stay visible. The head-only mark needs no disc.
  • An in-app name ("Piko") is optional and undecided. Never print a name with the logo.

10 · A cleaner app

Remove before you add

The re-skin changes how the app looks, never what it does. These are the rules for every screen.

One job per screen

Lead with the single main action. Demote the rest to secondary or text buttons, or a menu.

Fewer surfaces

Paper, raised cards, one border style. No nested cards, decorative gradients or card shadows.

Numbers first

Scores and streaks in the stat style with a short label. Let the number carry the screen.

Plain words

Rewrite labels to the voice table. Replace instruction paragraphs with clear labels.

Same parts everywhere

Every button, chip, meter and card comes from the components above. No one-off styles.

Accessible by default

4.5:1 text in both themes, visible focus rings, 44 px targets, real buttons and labels.
